SALES POLICY

Customer Enquiries:
All customer enquiries should be recorded with customer name, contact details, service requirements, and enquiry date.

Lead Prioritization:
Leads should be prioritized based on:
- Customer budget
- Business requirements
- Engagement level
- Response time
- Purchase intention

Lead Follow-up:
Sales representatives should follow up with potential customers regularly.

Quotation Policy:
A quotation should be prepared after understanding the customer's requirements and project scope.

Customer Communication:
Communication should be professional, clear, and customer-friendly.

Discount Policy:
Any discount must be approved by the authorized business representative.

Important:
The AI assistant may recommend follow-up actions, but it must not approve discounts, confirm quotations, or send customer commitments without human approval.
